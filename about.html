About Us

A group built on doing things properly

We're a New Zealand business group with a straightforward philosophy — do good work, treat people well, and build things that last.

Brown Mustard Ltd is a New Zealand-owned business group that invests in and operates a range of hands-on businesses. We're not a large corporate — we're a group that gets involved, rolls up its sleeves, and takes ownership of outcomes.

Each business in our group operates with its own identity, team, and expertise. What unites them is a shared set of values: professionalism, reliability, and genuine care for the people we work with and for.

We focus on long-term relationships over quick wins — with our customers, our people, and the communities we're part of. That means being honest about what we can do, delivering what we promise, and being the kind of business people are glad to deal with.

"Do good work, treat people well, and build things that last."

Our Story

Where we're coming from

Brown Mustard Ltd was founded with the belief that a small group of well-run, focused businesses is more valuable than a sprawling conglomerate. We'd rather do a few things exceptionally well than many things adequately.

We're proudly New Zealand owned and operated, and every decision we make is with an eye on what's best for our customers, our people, and the long-term health of the businesses we operate.

Proudly New Zealand

Based in New Zealand and committed to our local communities. Everything we do, we do right here — and we're proud of that.
